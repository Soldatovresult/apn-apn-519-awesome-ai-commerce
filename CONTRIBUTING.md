# Contributing to Awesome AI Commerce

Thanks for helping build the best list of AI + commerce tooling! 🙌

## How to add something

1. Fork this repo
2. Add your entry to the right section in `README.md`
3. Use the format: `[Name](https://link) — short, factual, one-line description.`
4. Keep entries alphabetical within a section where possible
5. Open a Pull Request with a short note on why it belongs

## What belongs here

✅ Tools, libraries, frameworks, and resources that combine **AI/automation** with **commerce** (selling, support, sourcing, ops, marketing).

✅ Open-source *and* commercial tools — as long as they're genuinely useful.

## What doesn't

❌ Dead / unmaintained projects
❌ Pure self-promotion with no value to readers
❌ Broken or affiliate-only links
❌ Anything unrelated to commerce + AI

## Quality bar

- Descriptions must be factual and under ~120 characters
- No marketing fluff ("revolutionary", "game-changing", etc.)
- Link must resolve (no 404s)

PRs that follow these get merged fast. Thank you for contributing! ⭐
